## Other sources and retention

OSV, PyPI, scanner output and vendor/distro references aggregate multiple
origins. GitHub's CC-BY-4.0 terms **do not cover every feed or upstream page**.
Do not infer a redistribution grant from availability, a successful scan or
an archive checksum. Follow original source notices and links in the dated
records. Full October20 vendor bodies remain private/archive-only; compact
identity/range projections do not authorize distributing that archive.

Public source preparation keeps historical evidence unchanged. The intended-
tree review includes decompressed material and flags unresolved source-specific
redistribution rights before import; it is not legal clearance for all feeds,
private archives or historical PR references. Non-public correspondence and
planning material are never intended public content.
